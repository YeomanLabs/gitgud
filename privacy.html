Git Gud

Privacy policy · Last updated November 8, 2026

Your data stays on your iPhone

Git Gud has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers. Your workouts, weigh-ins, food log, goals and settings are stored on your iPhone and in your own device backups. Nobody else, including the developer, can see them.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, Git Gud saves your workouts, weigh-ins and the food you log to Health, and reads your weight and the food other apps save there. Health data is only used to show your training and food in the app. It is never sold, shared, used for advertising or sent anywhere by Git Gud. You can change what Git Gud can read or save at any time in the Health app.

Barcode lookups

When you scan or type a barcode, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a free food database, to look the product up. Their privacy policy covers that request: openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to scan barcodes and to photograph nutrition labels. Labels are read on your iPhone, and the photos aren't kept or sent anywhere. When you choose a photo instead, Git Gud only sees the one you pick.

Reminders

Workout reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server.

Children

Git Gud isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything it stored. Anything it saved to Apple Health stays in Health until you delete it there.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on the web, with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the support page.
