Git Gud

A dark-fantasy workout and food log for iPhone

Git Gud gets you to the gym three times a week. Every workout raises your level, heavy lifts beat bosses, and your daily calories are a flask that drains as you eat.

Get help

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

Where is my data? On your iPhone only. There's no account and nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes its data.

Why isn't my food from another app showing? Connect Apple Health in Settings, then allow the other app to save nutrition to Health. Food other apps save is added to today's totals.

A barcode wasn't found. Barcodes are looked up on Open Food Facts, a free, community-built database. If a product is missing, add it from its label once (or photograph the label) and scanning it will find it next time.

How does weight go up? Finish every set with every target rep and the next workout adds weight. Miss three times in a row and it drops 10%.
